How to Convert an Image to PNG

  1. Choose Image: Select or drag your source images into the converter.
  2. Browser Processing: Your browser decodes the image locally in memory.
  3. Lossless PNG Encoding: Encodes pixel data using standard DEFLATE compression.
  4. Instant Download: Download individual PNG files or all as a ZIP package.
